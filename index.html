Local Journal

Your journal. Your device. Nobody else.

Local Journal is a private, local-first offline diary app that stores everything on your device. No cloud sync, no accounts, no ads, and no in-app tracking. Whether you journal daily or occasionally, Local Journal gets out of the way and lets you write.

Features

Built for people who value privacy

01

Local First

Everything stays on your device. No cloud sync, no servers, no way for anyone else to read your entries.

02

Voice to Text

Speak naturally and your words are transcribed instantly — powered by Whisper AI. Premium feature.

03

No Accounts

No sign-up, no login, no email. Open the app and start writing. It's that simple.

04

App Lock

Protect your journal with your device's existing PIN or biometrics. No separate password to remember.

05

Search & Timeline

Find any entry instantly with full-text search. Browse your history in a clean, chronological timeline.

06

Export Your Data

Text export is always free. PDF and JSON export available with premium. Your data is always yours. When you save or share a backup (text, PDF, or JSON), you choose where it goes — keep it on your device, or send it to your own cloud storage like iCloud Drive or Google Drive. We believe you should be deliberate with your personal data and decide, each time you save or share.

Privacy

Designed to be private by default

Local Journal does not collect personal data, does not require accounts, and does not transmit your journal entries anywhere. What you write stays with you — always.
